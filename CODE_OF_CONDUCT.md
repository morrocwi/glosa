# Code of Conduct

This project follows a short, plain-language conduct standard.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. Critique claims and
  evidence, not the person making them.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language, in any
  language, in any issue, PR, comment, or discussion.
- Refer to people by role, not name, unless they are the named author of
  the work or have publicly attached their own name to the specific
  contribution being discussed.
- Do not post unverifiable negative or accusatory claims about a named
  person, family, or organization. If a structural relationship needs
  stating and no verifiable source exists, state only the structural
  relationship, without the accusation.
- Do not share anyone's private information (address, phone number,
  credentials, non-public identifying details) without their explicit
  consent.
- Maintainers may edit, hide, or lock content that violates this standard,
  and may block repeat violators from the repository.

Reports of conduct violations may be opened as a GitHub issue if the report
itself contains no private information, or otherwise sent privately to the
author's public contact line in `.zenodo.json` / `CITATION.cff`.

This is a small, standalone-scholar project without a formal enforcement
committee. The founder makes the final call on any conduct matter,
consistent with the standpoint and authority described in `AGENTS.md`.
