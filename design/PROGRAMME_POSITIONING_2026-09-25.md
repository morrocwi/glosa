## 1. Core axis

The programme's central line, as the founder states it:

AI Assistance → Semantic/Cognitive Change → Human Return → Retained Human Capability → Human Conversion

Two separations carry the axis:

- **Influence ≠ Warrant.** Being moved by an AI output is not the same as having grounds for the
  belief. *Toledo status:* not found as a registered relation under this wording in the Toledo
  registry snapshot read for this note (public `origin/main`, 2026-09-20). Related registered
  separations exist (`A.5/H.07.v1` includes amplification ≠ warrant; `A.5/H.10.v1` includes
  provenance ≠ warrant) — related, not the same. Stated here in words only.
- **Assisted Performance ≠ Retained Human Capability.** Nearest registered form: `A.5/H.08.v1`
  (assisted performance ≠ unaided Human Return; `ΔPerformance_AI>0 ⇏ ΔH_return>0`, where Human
  Return is a six-field tuple, `H.16`, not a capability scalar); the identification with retained
  capability is stated in words only, not yet in Toledo (PROPOSAL). See also `A.5/W.13.v1`
  (assisted performance ≠ human learning) and `EQ-015/S.50.v1` (coupled performance ≠ Human
  Return).

## 2. Ceiling ladder

Each rung names what the work would have to contain, not a venue. The founder's notes associate
rungs with particular journal tiers; that association is **RELAYED, NEEDS_VERIFICATION** and is
not recorded as a target.

1. **Conceptual architecture.** A clearly bounded architecture of constructs and their relations.
   The founder's reading: achievable now.
2. **Theory-building / integrative theory.** Needs, for every construct, a definition; a justified
   mechanism; boundary conditions (where it does not occur); explicit propositions; rival
   explanations; and observations that would refute each proposition. The founder's example
   relation — Human Return depending on semantic/cognitive change, retention, agency, warrant and
   context — is **not registered in Toledo as written** and is kept in words here; the registered
   Human Return objects it would have to reuse are `EQ-015/H.16.v1` (six-field audit tuple) and
   `EQ-015/H.17.v1` (four-field tuple). The recording system for this rung is
   `methodology/P26_claim_evidence_theory_matrix.md`.
3. **Field-level perspective / roadmap.** The argument that the field may be measuring human–AI
   interaction in the wrong unit, and should read three quantities separately: assisted
   performance, retained capability, and converted capability. The first separation is registered
   (`A.5/H.08.v1`); a registered three-way separation of these exact quantities was not found in
   the snapshot read — stated here in words only.
4. **Formal + executable theory.** Concept → formal specification → executable model → falsifier
   → benchmark → reproducible artifact.
5. **Research programme.** The rungs above held together, each with its own records.

## 4. Not yet claimed

The programme does **not** claim, now, "a general theory of human–AI intelligence". Any sentence
in a glosa artifact that reads as that claim fails the overclaim scan (`P10` R6).

## 5. Weakest bridge

Semantic/Cognitive Change → Human Return → Human Conversion. For this bridge to hold it must become
operationalisable (each quantity read by a declared instrument) and falsifiable (a stated
observation that would break each arrow). Until then every claim that crosses it stays at the tier
of its weakest arrow.
