Core Claim
Central proposition. Human-AI dialogue is not merely information exchange. The architecture begins from a phenomenological
floor: meanings appear as context- and time-situated; meanings depend on relations; dialogue can change what becomes
accessible next; repeated traversal can make a route easier to re-enter; and exposure does not guarantee retention. The formal
architecture introduced later is an attempt to preserve these distinctions without identifying the phenomena with any one model.
Within a bounded session, Human and AI-under-current-context can recursively alter one another's accessible semantic routes
and accumulate reciprocal momentum; that amplification is epistemically neutral. Across the session boundary, only the human
is permitted a retained update in the present scope. Independent resistance determines whether candidate structures remain
corrigible. The decisive question is therefore what becomes easier to think, what persists, and what can still be corrected.

1. The Question
The central design question is: under what architecture can repeated human-AI dialogue enlarge a human being's reachable,
discriminable, corrigible space of inquiry rather than merely accelerate output, stabilize a preferred frame, or transfer epistemic
work to the system?

3. Why Dialogue Matters: Language as Epistemic Transport
The mechanism is not 'AI gives more information.' The internal programme treats language as part of epistemic mediation:
articulation makes distinctions nameable, comparable, transportable, and revisable. A human may begin with tacit experience,
intuition, an unresolved residual, or a partially formed problem. Language externalizes only a bounded readout of that state, but
once articulated it becomes available for transformation.
                                                              Z_H,t ⟶(L_H) Q_H,t
Here Z_H,t is the human's retained state - experience, assumptions, stakes, partial knowledge, unresolved difference - and L_H
is linguistic articulation. The question Q_H,t is not identical to the whole human state; it is a bounded readout of it.
AI then acts as a low-cost transport and perturbation system. It can decompose the question, rename distinctions, search
analogies, reverse causal direction, generate counterexamples, bridge domains, and construct rival explanations.
                                                   Q_H,t ⟶(T_AI) {Q_i,t+1, K_like_i,t}
The crucial object is not the polished sentence but the set of new distinctions that become reachable. K_like denotes knowledge-
like candidates: outputs with the form of explanation, argument, synthesis, or hypothesis, but without automatic validated status.

4. Phenomenological Ground: What the Architecture Is Trying to Explain
The architecture does not begin from the claim that meaning is atomic, molecular, chemical, computational, or low-rank. It
begins from a smaller phenomenological set: words and expressions appear with context-dependent meanings; meanings occur
through relations; repeated relations can become easier to re-enter; dialogue can reorganize the horizon from which a later
question is generated; and some reorganizations persist while others disappear. Formalism comes after these appearances and
remains answerable to them.
                    Phenomenology → Structural architecture → Formal / metaphorical compression
This ordering is methodological. Phenomenology anchors what must be explained; structural architecture exposes relations
among the distinctions; metaphor compresses the structure for thought and communication. None is permitted to substitute for
the others.
4.1 P1 - Meaning Is Temporally Situated
The same surface expression can appear differently to the same person after experience. The word has not changed, but the
horizon from which it is interpreted may have changed.
                 surface(u, τ1) = surface(u, τ2) does not imply Meaning_H(u, τ1) = Meaning_H(u, τ2)
4.2 P2 - Meaning Is Relational
Meaning is not exhausted by isolated lexical content. What an expression does in inquiry depends partly on the relations
through which it is read - for example freedom-with-choice, freedom-with-responsibility, or freedom-under-oppression.
                 Meaning(u_i) is partly conditioned by Relations(u_i, u_j, ... | reader, context, question)
4.3 P3 - Conversation Changes What Becomes Thinkable Next
A response can make a distinction, analogy, objection, or route available that was not salient in the previous turn. The next
question can therefore arise from a changed field of accessibility even before any durable learning is established.
                                 Q_t → Response_t → AccessibleRoutes_{t+1} → Q_{t+1}
4.4 P4 - Repeated Traversal Can Create Inclination
When a conversational route is repeatedly traversed, it can become easier to re-enter. This is the phenomenological anchor of
semantic momentum. It does not imply that the route is warranted or true.
                               Repeated traversal → easier re-entry (not → greater warrant)
4.5 P5 - Exposure Does Not Imply Retention
Some interactions pass through with little durable consequence; others alter later interpretation, questioning, or action. Any
architecture of AI-assisted human expansion must therefore separate exposure from retained change.
                                             Exposure ≠ Retention ≠ Improvement
4.6 Graceful Degradation Principle
A robust concept paper should survive loss of any non-essential metaphor. If atom, bond, molecule, catalyst, fusion, LoRA, or
nuclear-reaction vocabulary is removed, the load-bearing architecture should remain: meaning-bearing readouts, relations,
context, history-dependent accessibility, reciprocal coupling, retention, provenance, and resistance. This is conceptual
robustness by graceful degradation.
                              Reduce machinery; preserve phenomena; preserve architecture.

5. Semantic Micro-Foundation: Contextual Semantic Atoms, Bonds, and Molecules
The phenomenological floor now permits a narrower formal question: what is the smallest task-relative semantic readout that
must be tracked for the present inquiry? The architecture calls such a unit a semantic atom only as optional shorthand. It is not
universally a word. A lived word is the default observable entry unit, but a phrase, construction, or concept label may be the
minimal readout-bearing unit when the task requires it.
                                      a^A_{u,τ} := R_A( u | H^A_τ, C_τ, Q_τ, P_τ )
Here u is the minimal unit selected for the declared task; A is the reader; H^A_τ is the reader's current semantic horizon; C is
context; Q is the active question; and P is provenance. R_A is a bounded readout, not a claim that meaning is stored as a
substance inside u.

5.1 Semantic Graph and Contextual Bondability
The load-bearing formal core is a reader-relative semantic graph, not chemistry. Let nodes be currently accessible semantic
readouts and edges be active relations under the declared context and question. 'Valence' is retained only as optional shorthand
for context-conditioned bondability.
                                         G^A_τ = (A_τ, E_τ, W_τ, C_τ, Q_τ, P_τ)
                                     Bondable^A_τ(i) := { j : W^A_{ij,τ}(C,Q) > b0 }
A_τ is the current node set, E_τ the active relation set, and W_τ the relation weights or directions. Bondability is context-
relative and task-relative; a larger neighborhood means more accessible relations, not greater truth.
5.2 Semantic Bonds as Optional Interpretive Compression
A semantic bond is simply an active graph relation with enough weight to matter for the current interpretation, hypothesis, or
question. Chemical labels are retained only where they compress graph properties that are independently stated.
   Covalent-like: a strongly mutual relation in which removing either direction materially weakens the carried distinction.
    Graphically, W_ij > 0 and W_ji > 0 with joint constitutive load.
   Ionic-like: a strongly asymmetric relation in which framing, prestige, or semantic influence flows mainly in one direction.
    Graphically, W_ij >> W_ji.
   Weak/contextual: a relation that is live under one context but decays or disappears under another. Graphically, W_ij(C1) >
    b0 while W_ij(C2) ≈ 0.
                     B^A_{ij,τ} is an interpretation of W^A_{ij,τ}; the graph relation is primary.
These labels are removable structural analogies. If future operationalization cannot distinguish them beyond ordinary graph-
edge properties, the chemistry vocabulary should be dropped while retaining the underlying relational graph.
5.3 Semantic Configurations (Molecule as Shorthand)
A concept, hypothesis, explanation, or local worldview can be represented as a finite semantic configuration: a subgraph of
meaning-bearing nodes and active relations under a reader and context. 'Molecule' is a compact metaphor for this relational
whole, not an additional ontology.
                                M^A_τ := G^A_τ[S_τ] for a task-relevant subgraph S_τ
Two statements may contain the same words but instantiate different semantic molecules because their bond topology, context,
provenance, or reader-relative meaning differs. Conversely, differently worded statements may collapse into one readout-
equivalent molecule if they license the same accessible distinctions and consequences for the current inquiry.
5.4 Human Temporalization: The Same Word Can Become a Different Future Atom
Humans are temporal knowers. Retained experience can alter not only beliefs at the sentence level but the future bondability
and salience of the words through which later situations are read. The present note therefore adds a human temporalization
operator:
                H^H_{s+1,0} = T_H( H^H_{s,0}, η_s ΔM^H_s, δ^world_{s:s+1}, X^other_{s:s+1} )
T_H is a cross-session human temporalization operator. Retained dialogue is only one input. World correction, other people,
later reading, memory reconstruction, bodily experience, and intervening events may also reshape the human semantic horizon.
The architecture therefore does not attribute all later change to AI.
                    Semantic momentum → retention gate → temporalized human semantic structure
The AI side remains asymmetrical by scope. Its contextual semantic states can reorganize within session, but the present model
does not carry AI momentum or AI semantic atoms across the session boundary:
                  m^{AI}_{s+1,0} = 0 and no cross-session AI semantic-atom persistence is modeled
5.5 AI as Semantic Transport and Optional Catalytic Metaphor
AI's distinctive leverage can now be stated at the atom-bond level. Translation, analogy, decomposition, counterexample, and
cross-domain search can make previously costly semantic transitions easier to access. Rather than importing literal activation
energy, the proposal reuses the existing switching-cost term from the semantic-mobility architecture:
         AI assistance may lower effective semantic switching cost: c_{A,t,Q}(e | AI) < c_{A,t,Q}(e | no-AI)

5.6 From Semantic Atom to Epistemic Chain Reaction
The chain-reaction metaphor now has an explicit micro-to-macro pathway. A human utterance exports a semantic molecule; AI
perturbs, decomposes, or recombines its bonds; the human receives candidate molecules; some relations are rejected, some
remain transient, and some are retained. Retained reorganization then changes the bondability of later human questions, which
can seed further AI branching.
    word-state → semantic atom → bond → semantic molecule → reciprocal reaction → momentum → retention →
                                          temporalized human reader
               M^H_{s,t} → M^{AI}_{s,t} → {M^cand_1,...,M^cand_n} → η_s ΔM^H_s → M^H_{s+1,0}
This micro-level graph supplies a mechanism for the existing epistemic multiplication factor k_epi from the earlier chain-
reaction work. No independent k_sem law is required: bond formation, bond breaking, and recombination are proposed
mechanisms by which new live readout-distinguishable child classes may arise.
           Semantic recombination is a micro-level mechanism under k_epi, not a second multiplication law.
5.7 Context Wisdom as Semantic-Bond Governance
The architecture also sharpens the earlier Context Wisdom programme. Context Wisdom is not possession of many meanings
and not vocabulary size. It is the capacity to recognize what a semantic atom is carrying here and now, which bonds are active,
where those bonds came from, which are context-dependent, which can survive resistance, and when a bond should be
loosened, broken, or reorganized.
           Context Wisdom = Governance(Readouts, Relations, Context, Time, Provenance, Resistance)
On this view, wisdom is partly bond governance: the disciplined ability to prevent inherited, prestigious, fluent, or repeatedly
traversed relations from becoming epistemically load-bearing without sufficient resistance.

6. The Conversational Epistemic Reaction
A conversation becomes an epistemic reaction when one turn changes the conditions under which the next turn is generated.
The relevant unit is therefore not a single prompt-response pair but a session-indexed sequence. Let s denote the conversation
session and t a turn within that session.
                                    H_{s,t} → AI_{s,t} → H_{s,t+1} → AI_{s,t+1} → …
Each side reads a bounded representation of the other side's previous output. Repeated traversal of related semantic routes can
make those routes easier to re-enter at the next turn. This is the point at which dialogue becomes history-shaped rather than
memoryless.
                                      m_{t+1}(e) = λ m_t(e) + 1[e_t = e], 0 ≤ λ < 1
This inherits the semantic-momentum idea from the hypothesis-generation work: momentum is a finite history score for
recently traversed semantic directions. It describes ease of re-entry, not warrant, truth, or durable learning. The Human-AI case
requires two coupled momentum states, one on each side of the conversation.
6.1 Expansion reaction
  Q_t[verify] → AI branching → K_like → distinct classes → resistance → retained correction → richer Q_t+1
If each retained frontier item can seed more than one new live, readout-distinguishable, minimally grounded child route, the
process behaves like a controlled epistemic chain reaction. The multiplication is productive only after redundancy and
unsupported candidates are pruned; raw output count is not the relevant quantity.
6.2 Tunnel reaction
   M_t → Q_t[confirm] → AI → K_like → candidate forgetting → assumed knowledge → M_t+1[more-rigid]
The same recursion can run in the opposite direction. Confirmation-oriented prompts preferentially solicit support for the
current frame; fluent candidates are assimilated as if already validated; repeated use increases attraction to the same semantic

basin; and later questions are generated from a more rigid model. The system can therefore produce more text, more confidence,
and less human epistemic range at the same time.
6.3 Reciprocal Semantic Momentum Inside the Session
Within a session, the human and the AI-under-current-context are mutually perturbing readers because each turn becomes part
of the other side's next input and of the ordered conversational lineage. Semantic momentum is therefore retained as a finite
history score on each side; reciprocal coupling itself is represented by the session-domain stepper, not by assumed cross-
coupling coefficients.
                                   m^H[s,n+1](e) = λ_H m^H[s,n](e) + 1[e^H[s,n] = e]
                                 m^AI[s,n+1](e) = λ_AI m^AI[s,n](e) + 1[e^AI[s,n] = e]
Here m^H and m^AI are finite history readouts for recently traversed semantic directions. They describe ease of re-entry, not
warrant or truth. The two histories become coupled because the next domain state is stepped using the current Human output,
AI output, context, and shared ordered lineage.
To remain native to Readout Genesis, let Z_dlg[s,n] be the declared finite dialogue-domain state at turn n, containing the
relevant Human readout, AI-under-current-session readout, active semantic relations, context, and lineage.
                         Z_dlg[s,n+1] = F_dlg#(Z_dlg[s,n], u_H[s,n], u_AI[s,n], c[s,n], T[s,n])
  This is a domain-level stepper under the Genesis finite-transition architecture, not a new root law. For a non-
  empty seed set Sigma[s,n] of retained/readout-visible distinctions, define D_recip[s,n,L] as the lineage-linked
  descendants that remain readout-distinguishable within the frozen finite horizon [n,n+L] and whose declared
              lineage contains at least one reciprocal cross-agent return (H->AI->H or AI->H->AI).

This diagnostic is finite, lineage-based, and readout-relative. It does not predict unbounded growth. It differs from k_epi:
chi_recip measures reciprocal semantic descendant gain before epistemic pruning, whereas k_epi concerns retained live,
readout-distinguishable, minimally grounded epistemic child classes after pruning.
Coupled Dialogue Proposition [Open].
If current-turn content is held approximately fixed, prior within-session traversal history should alter the distribution of next-
turn semantic transitions for at least some dialogue classes. If no such history effect remains after current-state and prompt
content are controlled, m^{AI}_{s,t} should be removed as a distinct object and the coupled model reduced accordingly.
    Human-AI dialogue is coupled when each side's retained/read context changes the other's next accessible
                                             semantic routes.
The proposition is deliberately stronger than saying that prompts influence outputs. It claims a recursive dependence of later
accessible routes on the trajectory already traversed inside the same session.
Crucially, amplification itself is epistemically neutral. A supercritical dialogue can amplify verification, rival generation,
defeaters, and cross-domain distinctions; or it can amplify confirmation, same-basin framing, candidate forgetting, and
rhetorical confidence. The reaction intensity and the epistemic direction must therefore be kept separate.
                                        Amplification ≠ Retention ≠ Warrant ≠ Truth
6.4 The Retention Gate: A Human-LoRA Account of What Survives the Session
The Human LoRA work supplies the persistence mechanism across the session boundary. Experience is not automatically an
update: only selected residues may be retained, consolidated, and integrated into the dispositions through which later situations
are interpreted and acted upon. Applied here, the AI side is deliberately restricted to session-bounded contextual momentum,
while only the human side is permitted a durable cross-session update.
                                    ΔΩ^H_s ≈ η_s B_s A_s,                  rank(B_s A_s) ≪ d_H
This is a functional LoRA-inspired description: B_s A_s represents a restricted human update in a task-relevant subspace, d_H
the wider operative organization, and η_s ∈ [0,1] the session-end human retention gate. It is not a claim that the brain literally
implements engineering LoRA. The same session can yield pass-through, transient perturbation, or retained human adaptation.

                                η_s ≈ 0 → Ω^H_{s+1,0} ≈ Ω^H_{s,0}                          (pass-through)
                η_s > 0 → Ω^H_{s+1,0} = Ω^H_{s,0} + η_s ΔΩ^H_s (retained human adaptation)
Retention is direction-neutral. A retained human update can increase corrigibility and future question quality, or stabilize
confirmation, candidate forgetting, and semantic fixation. The AI-side momentum does not survive the session boundary in this
model:
                                   m^{AI}_{s+1,0} = 0          (by architectural scope definition)
This reset is a constitutive scope boundary of the present theory. The AI-side momentum object exists only for the bounded
session being modeled. Persistent memory, personalization, fine-tuning, external state, or cross-session agent memory are
excluded from this object and would require a different architecture. The asymmetry is therefore intentional: reciprocal
momentum is modeled within session, but only the human may carry a retained epistemic residue into the next session.
                  AI carries the dialogue within the session; the human may carry its residue beyond it.
                                        Dialogue is exposure; retention is adaptation.
                                         Retention magnitude ≠ epistemic direction.

7. The Epistemic Entry Condition: Before the First AI Response
The programme's earlier formulations were strongest during and after interaction. A standalone design principle requires one
additional layer: the human should establish an epistemic anchor before exposure to the first AI answer. The proposed entry
state is:
                                              H_0 = (P_0, M_0, U_0, E_0, Φ_0)
 P_0 - the problem the human is trying to understand, stated in the human's own terms.
 M_0 - the present model, explanation, or working belief before AI exposure.
 U_0 - what remains unknown, ambiguous, or unresolved.
 E_0 - evidence or records already available independently of the coming AI response.
 Phi_0 - a declared change condition: what observation, argument, or result would make the human revise M_0.
The minimum entry gate is then:
                                          ENTRY_H→AI = H_0 ∧ V_0 ∧ C_0 ∧ R*
where:
    V_0 - verification intent: use AI to seek rivals, defeaters, hidden assumptions, boundary conditions, and discriminating
     observations rather than merely support for M_0.
 C_0 - candidate contract: before seeing the answer, accept that AI output begins as K_like, not as certified knowledge.
 R* - precommitted resistance route: specify how an important candidate could be checked by a source, record, experiment,
     independent critic, domain authority, or other sufficiently independent route.
For deliberate human epistemic expansion, these are treated as entry requirements of the proposed architecture: a visible human
baseline, verification intent, a candidate-status contract, and at least one precommitted route of resistance. They are not claimed
to be necessary for every casual or low-stakes use of AI; they are necessary for the specific target defined here - auditable
expansion rather than untracked influence.

8. Candidate Status and the Critical Shortcut
                                          AI(Q) = K_like,            K_like ≠ K_validated
The candidate contract is central because form and status are different variables. A generated response may contain structure,
citations, academic language, or an apparently coherent explanation while remaining only a provisional candidate.
Candidate forgetting is the shortcut in which that provisional status is lost:
                                         K_like → K_assumed                (validation bypass)
Once this bypass occurs, the dialogue can feed its own outputs back as premises. The system then accumulates apparent support
without a corresponding increase in independent constraint. This is why provenance and resistance are not optional decorations:
they are what keep the generative loop from becoming self-certifying.

Constitutive Necessity Proposition [Derived].
Define durable human epistemic expansion as an increase in the human capacity to reach at least one non-equivalent live
alternative, remain answerable to possible correction, and carry some correction into later inquiry. Then D > 0, R > 0, and A > 0
are necessary.
                                D = 0 ⇒ no new epistemic class beyond the retained frame
                  R = 0 ⇒ no principled separation between expansion and self-confirming proliferation
                     A = 0 ⇒ possible system gain, but no demonstrated durable human-potential gain
The necessity is constitutive rather than empirical: it follows analytically from the stipulated target concept of durable human
epistemic expansion. None of D, R, or A is sufficient alone, and none guarantees truth.
                             D > 0 is necessary for expansion beyond the existing readout class.
                                R > 0 is necessary for corrigibility and world-answerability.
                  A > 0 is necessary for any claimed expansion of the human rather than the system alone.

10. Master Direction Equation
The full architecture separates three orthogonal questions: within-session amplification, cross-session human retention, and
epistemic direction. Let the session reaction state be:
                             R_s^epi = (chi_recip[s,L], η_s, Δ_s),                  where Δ_s := G_s - T_s
Here chi_recip[s,L] describes finite reciprocal lineage amplification within the declared session horizon; η_s describes how
much residue persists in the human; and Δ_s describes the direction of that retained pressure. These terms are deliberately not
collapsed into a single score.
                         Durable human direction: sign(ΔP_H(s)) = sign(η_s Δ_s), with η_s ≥ 0
chi_recip[s,L] is not multiplied into epistemic value because a strongly amplifying reciprocal lineage can magnify either
disciplined verification or self-confirming closure. Reaction intensity and epistemic direction are different objects.
     η_s - human session-end retention gate: the fraction of the conversational residue that becomes durable enough to alter later
      interpretation or questioning.
     k_s - candidate multiplication generated during the session after retained frontier items seed further search.
     d_s - proportion of generated candidates that are genuinely readout-distinct rather than paraphrastic duplicates.
     v_s - maintained verification orientation during the session.
     p_s - provenance visibility across data, source text, human assumptions, inference, and AI synthesis.
     r_s - effective independent resistance.
     f_s - candidate-forgetting rate.
     a_s - agency-preserving integration quality: whether retained change remains owned, reconstructable, and usable by the
      human rather than merely delegated to the system.
     c_s - confirmation-seeking pressure.

                                                                                         EPISTEMIC NOTE | HUMAN-AI READOUT PROGRAMME
     b_s - same-basin reinforcement or semantic self-repetition.
     o_s - epistemic outsourcing of problem-framing, questioning, evaluation, or responsibility.
                                         G_s = g(k_s, d_s, v_s, p_s, r_s, 1-f_s, a_s)
                                                     T_s = h(c_s, f_s, b_s, o_s)
The functions g and h are dependency maps rather than calibrated scales. Where variables are defined on preregistered ordered
scales, the architecture requires only finite order-preserving comparisons: for example, holding all other declared inputs fixed,
increasing effective resistance from r1 to r2 with r2 >= r1 should not decrease G, and increasing confirmation pressure from c1
to c2 with c2 >= c1 should not decrease T. No differential form is required.
    Illustrative parameterizations may be multiplicative; the architecture itself does not require product-form necessity.
              η_s > 0 and G_s > T_s → Durable Expansion | η_s > 0 and G_s < T_s → Durable Tunnel
For retained episodes (η_s > 0), G_s = T_s is the proposed directional boundary. The separate within-session quantity
chi_recip[s,L] characterizes finite reciprocal lineage amplification, not epistemic value. Thus chi_recip[s,L] can be high while
ΔP_H(s) is positive, negative, or near zero. No term is yet an empirically calibrated universal scale; these equations are
dependency maps for future testing.
                      η_s ≈ 0, G_s > T_s → fleeting insight / no demonstrated durable expansion
                          η_s ≈ 0, G_s < T_s → fleeting distortion / little durable contraction
                                   η_s > 0, G_s > T_s → durable epistemic expansion
                                      η_s > 0, G_s < T_s → durable epistemic tunnel
The architecture therefore separates three questions that must not collapse:
    chi_recip[s,L] : How many readout-distinguishable reciprocal descendants does the declared seed set generate
                                         within the finite session horizon?
                               η_s : How much of the session persists in the human afterward?
                       sign(G_s - T_s) : In which epistemic direction does the retained change point?

11. The Human Return Test
The programme's target is not a better current answer by itself. The Human Return Test is a test of retained explicit epistemic
agency, not an exhaustive measure of all human learning. It asks what the human can reconstruct, discriminate, and question
once the conversational context is no longer doing the work for them.
     What did I think before the dialogue?
     Which distinction or rival did I encounter that I did not previously retain?
     What evidence, objection, or returned record changed my view?
     Which live rival remains unresolved?
     What is my next question, and why is it better formed than the previous one?
                                                 ΔC_system > 0 ⇏ ΔP_H > 0
If the system produces a superior answer but the human cannot reconstruct the revision, identify the remaining uncertainty, or
generate a better next question, the interaction may have increased system capability without demonstrating expansion of
human epistemic potential.

12. Derived Findings from the Internal Series
F1. Language is epistemic transport. Language does not merely report a finished thought. It makes distinctions portable
across questions, domains, and later rounds of revision.
F2. Dialogue is a recursive epistemic mechanism. Each side's output changes the semantic paths available to the other at the
next turn; repeated reciprocal perturbation can therefore create within-session conversational amplification.
F3. AI is a candidate multiplier, not a certifier. Its strongest leverage is cheap semantic transport, decomposition, analogy,
counterexample, and branching; none confers truth-status by itself.
F4. Output multiplicity is not epistemic multiplicity. Many sentences can collapse into one readout-equivalent class;
verbosity is not expansion.
F5. Question policy can reverse the direction of the same AI. Verification-oriented use exposes the model to defeat;
confirmation-oriented use can feed the model back into itself.
F6. Candidate forgetting is a central transition hazard. When K_like is silently upgraded to assumed knowledge, the
validation layer is bypassed and self-reinforcement becomes easy.
F7. World/independent resistance must remain outside the generative loop. A closed AI loop can critique itself but cannot
thereby become an unmediated certifier.
F8. Human potential is a return condition. Successful augmentation requires an epistemically productive residue to survive the
session boundary in the human, because AI-side momentum is reset by definition in this architecture.
F9. Retention is direction-neutral. Durable human change can consolidate corrective distinctions or consolidate confirmation,
candidate forgetting, and semantic fixation; durable change alone is not evidence of improvement.
F10. Reciprocal momentum is session-bounded. Human and AI outputs can mutually reshape semantic accessibility during a
session, but the AI-side momentum is not carried into the next session in this model.
F11. Reaction intensity is not epistemic direction. A high finite reciprocal-lineage gain can support either disciplined expansion
or a supercritical tunnel. chi_recip[s,L] therefore cannot be read as warrant, truth, or human benefit.
F12. The deepest temporal asymmetry is cross-session persistence. During a session, Human and AI can mutually reshape
accessible semantic routes; after the boundary, the AI-side momentum is set to zero by scope, while human retained residue
may alter the next session's starting reader.
F13. The semantic atom is task-relative, not universally lexical. A lived word is the default entry unit, but the minimal meaning-
bearing readout may be a phrase, construction, or concept label depending on the question and reader.
F14. Semantic bondability is contextual and graph-relative. Effective relation neighborhoods change with question, history,
social frame, retained experience, and reader; connectivity is not warrant.
F15. Human temporalization can alter future semantic accessibility. Retained dialogue can contribute to later changes, but world
events and other experiences remain co-determining inputs.
F16. AI catalysis acts on accessibility. AI can lower the effective switching cost between semantic regions through translation,
analogy, decomposition, and cross-domain bridging; easier access does not establish warrant.
F17. Concepts and hypotheses are relational configurations. Their working identity depends on graph structure and accessible
consequences, not merely lexical novelty; molecule language is optional shorthand for this structure.
F18. Micro-level semantic recombination and session-level reciprocal lineage gain are distinct. Semantic recombination
proposes a mechanism for generating new structures; chi_recip[s,L] measures lineage-traced reciprocal descendants before
epistemic pruning; k_epi remains the post-pruning epistemic multiplication concept. None is a truth score.
F19. Context Wisdom is semantic-bond governance. Wisdom includes the capacity to inspect, test, loosen, preserve, or
reorganize meaning relations under changing context, time, provenance, and resistance.
F20. Phenomenology is the load-bearing floor. If chemistry, LoRA, fusion, or dynamical metaphors are removed, the
architecture should still preserve the phenomena of temporal meaning, relational meaning, history-shaped accessibility,
selective retention, and corrigibility.

13. Practical Protocol: How to Enter and Use an AI Conversation
1. Anchor before AI. Write P_0, M_0, U_0, E_0, and Phi_0 before reading the first answer when the inquiry matters.
2. Ask to verify, not to confirm. Request serious rivals, failure conditions, hidden assumptions, counterexamples, and
observations that would discriminate alternatives.

3. Keep AI output typed. Default label: knowledge-like candidate. Do not let formatting, confidence, or fluency silently
change epistemic status.
4. Force real difference. Ask whether new outputs are genuinely non-equivalent or merely rephrasings of the same
consequence.
5. Preserve provenance. Keep visible what came from source records, human assumptions, AI synthesis, inference, and later
augmentation.
6. Precommit resistance. Identify at least one route able to say no before a candidate becomes load-bearing.
7. Manage reciprocal momentum. Treat repetition as a causal design variable, not neutral conversation history. If both sides
repeatedly traverse one semantic basin, deliberately inject a rival formulation, independent record, explicit falsifier, or fresh-
session reset before further amplification.
8. Check the human retention gate. Do not assume that exposure produced learning. Ask what distinction, evidence, or revision
the human can still reconstruct without replaying the AI transcript.
9. Audit the direction of retention. A durable update can still be harmful if it reinforces confirmation, same-basin attraction,
candidate forgetting, or outsourcing. Retention must be evaluated by direction, not magnitude alone.
10. Audit semantic bonds, not only sentences. Ask which words or concepts have become newly linked, whether the linkage is
symmetric or prestige-driven, whether it survives context removal, and what evidence could break the bond.
11. Alternate expansion and contraction. Expand broadly during search; contract aggressively during appraisal, decision,
publication, or high-stakes use.

14. Removal and Falsification Conditions
A conceptual architecture should lose components when those components cease to do explanatory work. The following are
explicit removal conditions:
   Remove m^{AI}_{s,t} as a distinct object if within-session path history adds no effect on next-turn semantic transitions
    once current prompt/content state is controlled.
   Remove m^H_{s,t} as a distinct object if human path-history effects disappear once current semantic state, salience, and
    task variables are controlled.
   Abandon or narrow the Human-LoRA bridge if retained conversational residues do not predict later selective changes in
    questioning, interpretation, or action beyond ordinary short-lived priming.
   Drop chi_recip[s,L] as a useful diagnostic if lineage-linked reciprocal descendants cannot be identified reproducibly under
    frozen reader, context, horizon, provenance, and readout-distinguishability rules, or if the ratio adds no information beyond
    ordinary turn count.
   Weaken the readout-distinctness requirement if raw candidate count predicts useful discovery just as well after redundancy
    and accessible consequence are controlled.
   Revise the D-R-A necessity claim if the target definition of durable human epistemic expansion is changed; under the
    present definition, a counterexample would require expansion without new distinction, corrigibility, or retained human
    change.
   Drop the atom vocabulary if task-relative minimal semantic readouts add no explanatory value beyond ordinary node
    notation; retain the underlying readout graph if it still does explanatory work.
   Drop covalent-like, ionic-like, and weak/contextual labels if they add no operational value beyond generic
    weighted/directed/context-sensitive graph edges; the graph core should remain intact.
   Narrow human temporalization if later semantic change is fully explained by current context and intervening world/other-
    person inputs without any retained contribution from the prior dialogue.

15. Claim Boundaries
   The phenomenological floor is descriptive, not infallible first-person proof. It identifies recurring appearances the
    architecture is designed to explain; empirical work may refine their scope, frequency, or mechanism.
   The semantic graph is load-bearing; atom, bond, molecule, catalyst, fusion, and chain-reaction terms are optional
    interpretive compression. The theory is intended to survive their removal.
   Atom, valence, bond, molecule, catalysis, fusion, and chain-reaction language are structural analogies layered over a
    graph/readout core. No claim is made that meaning is chemical substance, that semantic relations are physical bonds, or
    that conversational change obeys chemical kinetics.
   The entry gate, D-R-A necessity proposition, coupled dynamics, retention bridge, and directional equations are conceptual
    architecture with explicit typing; none is presented as an already validated universal psychological law.

16. One-Sentence Thesis
Use AI to multiply what can be questioned, not to multiply reasons for believing what you
                                      already think.
Human beings should enter AI dialogue with a retained baseline and verification intent. The architecture begins from
phenomenology rather than from chemistry: meanings are temporal and relational, dialogue changes accessibility, repetition can
create inclination, and exposure may or may not be retained. A graph/readout model makes those relations explicit; atom-bond-
molecule language compresses them without becoming load-bearing ontology. Within the session, Human and AI-under-
current-context may recursively alter one another's accessible routes; independent resistance must remain able to break
unsupported relations, while only retained human reorganization counts as durable augmentation.
