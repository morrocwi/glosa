# Idea note: ScientistTwo and the human epistemic residual

**Observation.** ScientistTwo demonstrates an autonomous ML research loop: from a human-specified problem, it establishes baselines, formulates hypotheses, runs experiments and ablations, writes the paper, and performs simulated peer-review/rebuttal without human intervention inside that loop.

**glosa implication.** System-level scientific performance and human retained capability must be evaluated separately:

`AI-generated knowledge ↑ ≠ human epistemic capability ↑`

The relevant human question is therefore not only *Can the system produce new knowledge?* but also *What can the human still independently understand, verify, reconstruct, and continue when AI support is removed?*

**Boundary.** The evidence is currently from machine-learning research tasks; the initial problem is human-specified, and the work is an arXiv preprint rather than independent confirmation that autonomous AI can replace scientific inquiry generally.

**Reference.** Nam, J., Yoon, J., Pan, Y., Wang, Y., Meng, R., Ranganathan, P., & Pfister, T. (2026). *ScientistTwo: Pioneering the Human Knowledge Frontier with Autonomous AI*. arXiv:2609.19644. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.19644
